This policy explains how Travelgames processes personal data when you use its mobile application for photographic challenges and competitions.

1. Data controller and contact

Controller: Ricardo Gracia Casado
Application: Travelgames
Privacy and contact email: travelgames.app@gmail.com

2. Data we process

  • Account data: email address, name and protected credentials.
  • Profile data you choose to provide, such as age, sex, country of birth and city of residence.
  • Device location, when you allow it, to check nearby challenges and competitions and, if you enable Explorer mode, send proximity alerts while the application is not in the foreground.
  • Photographs you take to complete challenges or participate in competitions.
  • If you report a photograph, the reason for the report and the minimum information needed to identify and review the content, including the name and internal identifier of the profile that posted it.
  • Votes, entries, points, results and technical data needed for security, operation and diagnostics.
  • For commercial challenges and campaigns, participation data and associated photographs, which are used to provide and moderate the service.

3. How we use data

  • To create and maintain your account and provide Travelgames features.
  • To check geographic proximity and resolve challenges that depend on location.
  • To verify photographs using automated image analysis systems.
  • To moderate competition photographs, create review collages and display approved photographs in the carousel and voting features of the relevant competition.
  • To receive and manually review photograph reports. The Travelgames team receives the reason, the image through a temporary private link and the profile data needed to identify the post. A report does not by itself cause the image to be removed or the account to be blocked.
  • To calculate points, rankings and winners, and send service-related communications.
  • To provide and monetise challenges, campaigns and services for third parties, and prepare aggregated performance statistics, such as the number of valid entries or unique participants.
  • To share only effectively anonymised statistics with third parties that commission, fund or participate in campaigns. We do not provide them with photographs, collages, names, identifiers, individual records or breakdowns that could identify participants.
  • To prevent fraud, technical abuse, duplicate accounts and uses that breach the Travelgames terms.

4. Photographs and encryption

Photographs are encrypted before they are sent from the application. The private decryption key is kept only in the backend. Challenge photographs are retained only when the challenge settings allow it; otherwise, they are deleted after evaluation. Competition photographs remain in quarantine until moderation and are deleted in accordance with the relevant period and monthly closing rules.

Photographs are used to provide Travelgames features, such as challenge verification, moderation and display within the application's features. They are not sold, licensed or provided to clients or sponsors as part of a paid campaign or service, and are not included in their reports or promotional materials. Third parties may pay for services to create or manage challenges and to receive aggregated statistics that have been effectively anonymised. We do not share individual data or breakdowns where there is a reasonable possibility of identifying a person.

How to report a competition photograph

  1. On the voting screen, find the photograph you want to report and tap the white thumbs-down icon in its upper-right corner.
  2. Read the notice and select Submit report to confirm, or Cancel if you do not want to send it.
  3. The application will confirm whether it received your report. If an error appears, you can try again. You can also email travelgames.app@gmail.com if you need to report a problem.

Use this option for inappropriate, abusive or potentially criminal content, not simply because you dislike a photograph. Reports are sent privately to Travelgames for review; they do not automatically remove the image or block the profile that posted it.

When a photograph is reported, Travelgames creates a temporary private link so the team can review it. The link expires after 24 hours; the photograph remains subject to the applicable retention period and competition closing process. The name and internal identifier of the profile that posted it are included in the notice to support manual review, but are not shown to the person making the report.

5. Service providers

We use technology providers to host the database, authenticate accounts, store photographs, send emails, translate content and carry out automated verification or moderation. These providers should process only the data needed to provide their services and are subject to their own policies and applicable legal safeguards.

Third parties that commission campaigns may receive effectively anonymised statistics, but not photographs or individual participant data. Technology providers may process photographs and other data only when necessary to host, protect or provide Travelgames on the application's behalf and in accordance with applicable safeguards.

6. Retention

We retain data while you maintain your account or for as long as it is needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve claims, prevent fraud or protect security. Photographs and competition results are retained under the rules for the relevant challenge or competition.

Photographs and personal data linked to an account are retained in accordance with the challenge rules and deleted when they are no longer needed or when the account is deleted, subject to the legal exceptions described in this policy. Effectively anonymised statistics may be retained without being linked to an individual account.

7. How to request deletion of your data or account

Request deletion of personal data

If you want to request deletion of personal data without deleting your account, email travelgames.app@gmail.com with the subject Request to delete personal data. State which data you want us to review and, where relevant, which account it relates to. Do not include your password or verification codes.

For security, we may ask you to confirm that you own the account before processing the request. We will review which data can be deleted and confirm the outcome. If some data must be retained due to a specific legal obligation or applicable legitimate need, we will explain the scope of that restriction where appropriate. If you want all data linked to your account deleted, request account deletion using the process below.

Request deletion of your account

You can request deletion of your Travelgames account without using the application. Email travelgames.app@gmail.com with the subject Request to delete Travelgames account.

  1. Write from the email address associated with your Travelgames account.
  2. State in your message that you want your account deleted. Do not include your password or verification codes.
  3. If you do not write from the registered email address or we need to confirm that you own the account, we will explain how to verify this before processing the request.
  4. Once verified, we will process the deletion and confirm by email when it is complete.

You can also start this process in the application under Profile → Delete account and confirm the action on screen.

Deletion removes the authentication account, profile, associated stored photographs and Travelgames data linked to the account, such as entries, votes and progress, where applicable. If a specific legal obligation or legitimate need relating to security, fraud prevention or the defence of claims requires us to retain data, we will limit it to what is necessary, keep it only for the applicable period, and delete or anonymise it when it is no longer needed. Statistics that are effectively anonymous cannot be linked to your account and may be retained.

8. Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection to the processing of your data by writing to travelgames.app@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority, including the Spanish Data Protection Agency where applicable.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational controls, including authentication, access rules, private storage and photograph encryption. No system connected to the Internet can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the application, providers or legal obligations change. We will publish the current version at this URL and state its update date.
